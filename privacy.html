Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Rekha for Meta Quest · Publisher: LOVNEO

Rekha is a mixed-reality tracing app. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: Rekha does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The app works offline and everything stays on your headset.

Information the app uses on your device

  • Passthrough and room layout. Rekha uses Meta Horizon OS features (passthrough and your saved room scan / Scene data) to place the guide on your walls and floors. Rekha does not access raw camera images; the room data is processed on the headset and is not sent anywhere by Rekha.
  • Hand and controller tracking. Hand and controller positions are used in real time to operate the menu and place the guide. They are not recorded or transmitted.
  • Images you choose. If you grant photo access, Rekha lists images stored on your Quest (for example in Download or Pictures) so you can pick one. Only images you select are copied into the app's private storage on your headset. Optional photo-to-sketch conversion runs entirely on the headset.
  • Settings. Preferences such as theme, opacity, sounds and haptics are saved locally on the headset.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking by the app, and no data sent to LOVNEO or third parties.
  • No in-app purchases or payment information.

Meta platform

Rekha runs on Meta Horizon OS and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may collect standard platform and diagnostic information under its own terms; see the Meta privacy policy. Rekha does not receive that information.

Removing your data

Because all data stays on your headset, uninstalling Rekha removes the images you imported into it and its settings. Images in your Quest's own folders are not changed by Rekha.

Children

Rekha does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

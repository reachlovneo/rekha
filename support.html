Support

Need help with Rekha, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

Open a request on GitHub: github.com/reachlovneo/rekha/issues. Please include your headset model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Quick answers

The guide is not on my wall

Run Space Setup in your Quest settings so Rekha knows where your walls and floor are, then use Style → Wall or Floor.

How do I open the menu?

Turn your left palm toward you, or press the Menu button on the left controller.

My photos don't appear

Allow photo access when asked. Images must be PNG or JPG in Download, Pictures or Screenshots, then tap Refresh.

Alignment feels off

Use Style → Calibrate with three small tape marks on the wall for the most accurate placement.
